# Yaprflow 5.2.10

This release improves AI output reliability across supported providers:

- OpenAI, OpenRouter, and Ollama responses now use provider-compatible output
  limits.
- Yaprflow automatically retries once with a larger response budget when a
  model stops before finishing.
- Meeting summaries and transcript actions have more room to complete longer,
  structured results.
- Provider errors remain concise and never expose the full service response.

Dictation, meeting recording, local storage, and privacy behavior are unchanged.
