# Yaprflow 5.2.0

Yaprflow now has two clear capture modes:

- **Quick Dictation** turns short speech into polished clipboard text from the
  menu bar or global shortcut.
- **Meeting Notes** privately captures microphone and Mac audio, combines the
  transcript with typed notes and calendar context, and creates structured,
  evidence-linked notes without a meeting bot or retained audio.

This release also adds saved meeting search, cross-meeting questions, templates,
editable generated notes, a cleaner mode-first menu, and a unified flat-red
app icon across Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

The companion iPhone and iPad 1.0 release provides Quick Dictation plus a
microphone-only **In-person Meeting** mode. Mobile meetings, notes, and Markdown
exports stay local to the device; other-app audio is not captured on iOS.
