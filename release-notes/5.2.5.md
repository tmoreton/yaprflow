# Yaprflow 5.2.5

This release restores reliable live transcription in Meeting Notes:

- Microphone capture again uses the proven raw-input path, avoiding a macOS
  voice-processing mode that could leave an active meeting with no transcript.
- Mac audio and microphone audio remain independent recognition streams.
- When Mac playback is also heard by the microphone, transcript reconciliation
  removes the duplicate from **Me** and retains the authoritative **Them** copy.
- The end-to-end meeting audio check now verifies the final, user-visible
  transcript across both capture streams.

Quick Dictation remains unchanged. Meeting transcription continues to run
locally, and raw meeting audio is not retained.
