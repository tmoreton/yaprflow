# Yaprflow 5.2.8

This maintenance release makes AI-generated meeting outputs more reliable:

- Action plans and meeting summaries now recover cleanly when an AI provider
  returns truncated or slightly malformed structured output.
- Structured output is rendered as native Yaprflow sections instead of exposing
  raw JSON in the meeting view.
- Existing saved meetings containing a raw structured response are repaired
  automatically when they are reopened.
- Unrecoverable structured output now produces a clear retry state rather than
  being displayed as meeting content.

Quick Dictation and the meeting transcription workflow are unchanged.
