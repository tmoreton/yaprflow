# Yaprflow 5.2.11

This release adds natural spoken punctuation to transcript polishing:

- Say “period” or “full stop,” “question mark,” or “exclamation point” to end
  a sentence and capitalize the next one.
- Say “comma,” “colon,” or “semicolon” to add inline punctuation.
- Say “new line” or “new paragraph” to format longer dictations.
- Clearly literal phrases such as “a difficult period in history” remain
  unchanged.

Spoken punctuation is available in Polished quick dictation and meeting
transcripts. Exact dictation mode continues to preserve the recognized words
verbatim.
