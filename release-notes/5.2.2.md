# Yaprflow 5.2.2

This release completes the unified Library experience for meetings and quick
dictations:

- Meeting output styles and reusable AI presets now share one clear generation
  workflow, while one-off questions remain available separately.
- Generated follow-ups are grounded in the transcript and no longer invent
  missing contact details such as email addresses.
- Live meeting transcripts are ordered consistently, use accurate timestamps,
  and remove duplicated system audio from the local-speaker track even when the
  two recognition streams phrase it differently.
- Meetings and dictations now share one output catalog containing General
  Summary, 1:1 Notes, Interview Notes, Research Synthesis, Sales Follow-up,
  Standup Update, Action Plan, Follow-up Email, and Detailed Notes. Each prompt
  can be edited from Settings.
- Quick dictations can now be deleted from the Library on both Mac and iPhone.
- Meeting and dictation controls are more consistent across the Mac and iOS
  apps, with clearer history and output handling.

Quick Dictation and Meeting Notes continue to transcribe locally, and raw
meeting audio is not retained.
