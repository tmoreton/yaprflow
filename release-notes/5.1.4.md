# Yaprflow 5.1.4

- Adds secure automatic updates to the direct-download Mac edition with
  Sparkle 2. Future releases can download and install from inside Yaprflow.
- Keeps the Mac App Store edition on Apple's update system, without Sparkle.
- Adds OpenAI, OpenRouter, and local Ollama choices for AI Summary while
  retaining Apple Intelligence on supported Macs.
- Adds in-app feedback and privacy-preserving usage and failure telemetry with
  an off switch in Settings.
- Refreshes the app icon and moves Send Feedback into Settings.
- Improves the paid website checkout, purchase terms, private download flow,
  browser voice demo, and product analytics.

Customers updating from 5.1.3 must download and install 5.1.4 from their
purchase page once. Automatic updates work after 5.1.4 is installed.
