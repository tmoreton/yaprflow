# Yaprflow 5.2.17

- Improves Meeting Notes speaker separation: Mac playback is less likely to
  appear as duplicate “Me” speech, while distinct microphone speech remains.
- Refines transcript matching for shortened or repeated system-audio echoes.
- Makes the menu bar recording indicator red for both meetings and Quick
  Dictation, with yellow reserved for preparation or paused states.
- Adds optional automatic polishing of new dictations using the selected AI
  provider. The original transcript and copied text remain unchanged.

Speech recognition remains on the Mac. If an online AI provider is selected
for optional polishing, that provider receives the dictation text.
