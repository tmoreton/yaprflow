# Yaprflow 5.2.18

- Simplifies Meeting Notes on Mac with a grouped Library sidebar, clearer
  recording controls, fewer competing actions, and a separate Settings window.
- Streamlines iPhone and iPad around two clear modes—Dictation and Meeting—with
  a persistent labeled capture control, one Library, and a two-column iPad
  meeting workspace.
- Standardizes labels such as Notes format and Your notes across Mac and iOS.
- Uses the same on-device Parakeet speech engine on Mac, iPhone, and iPad while
  removing unused legacy transcription runtimes and calendar integration.
- Improves meeting and dictation persistence, including safer recovery from
  interrupted writes and more consistent local records.
- Improves OpenRouter and OpenAI transcript processing when a model exhausts
  its response budget. Yaprflow retries with more room, then divides long
  transcripts into smaller parts if needed.
- Avoids decrypting saved AI credentials merely to open Settings or Meeting
  Notes, preventing unnecessary Keychain prompts and window stalls.

Speech recognition remains on-device and raw audio is not saved. The original
transcript remains unchanged. Text is sent off-device only when you choose and
use an online AI provider.
