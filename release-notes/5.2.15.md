# Yaprflow 5.2.15

This release makes recording feedback more compact and consistent:

- The Quick Dictation audio-level overlay is now 320 × 44 points, with less
  empty space around the waveform. Longer errors briefly expand the overlay
  so their message remains readable.
- The menu bar icon is neutral while idle, yellow while preparing or finishing,
  and red throughout a Quick Dictation or Meeting Notes recording session,
  including a paused meeting.

This build also includes the speech-boundary and transcription accuracy
improvements from 5.2.14. Speech recognition remains on the Mac.
