# Yaprflow 5.2.4

This release improves speaker separation in Meeting Notes:

- Microphone audio now uses Apple's system voice processing to remove Mac
  speaker playback before transcription.
- Mac audio remains an independent, authoritative `Them` source, while local
  microphone speech remains `Me`.
- Transcript-level echo reconciliation remains as a fallback for recognition
  differences and unusual audio-device configurations.
- Meeting capture reports a clear error when the selected microphone cannot
  support echo cancellation instead of silently mixing speakers.

Quick Dictation remains unchanged. Meeting transcription continues to run
locally, and raw meeting audio is not retained.
