# Yaprflow 5.2.22

- Prevents meeting capture from unnecessarily lowering the volume of other
  people speaking on the Mac.
- Makes repeated manual update checks safe while another update check is
  already running.
- Hardens audio processing, meeting-note editing, storage, and AI requests so
  unexpected input produces a recoverable error instead of closing the app.

Speech recognition remains on-device and raw audio is not saved.
