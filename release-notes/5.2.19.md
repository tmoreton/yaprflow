# Yaprflow 5.2.19

- Makes Meeting tools more compact while keeping summary generation, questions,
  provider status, and privacy information accessible.
- Shows saved meetings and dictations together in newest-first order, with
  shorter previews so recent transcripts are easier to find.
- Keeps the red recording and yellow preparation menu bar waveform the same
  size as the neutral icon.

Speech recognition remains on-device and raw audio is not saved. Text is sent
off-device only when you choose and use an online AI provider.
