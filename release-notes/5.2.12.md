# Yaprflow 5.2.12

This release upgrades on-device Mac transcription and adds natural spoken
punctuation:

- Quick Dictation and Meeting Notes now use NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 for
  substantially faster, highly accurate offline transcription.
- Parakeet automatically detects 25 supported European languages without a
  manual language setting.
- Dictation is finalized at natural pauses, while long meetings are processed
  in bounded sections so one- and two-hour sessions do not accumulate audio in
  memory.
- Meeting Notes continue to separate microphone speech from Mac system audio
  and remove system-audio echo from the “Me” transcript.
- In Polished mode, spoken commands such as “period,” “question mark,”
  “comma,” “new line,” and “new paragraph” produce natural formatting.
- Exact dictation mode continues to preserve recognized words verbatim.

All speech recognition remains on the Mac. Audio is not uploaded for
transcription.
