# Yaprflow 5.2.14

This release improves transcription accuracy at speech and section boundaries:

- Quick Dictation and Meeting Notes now retain more audio before and after
  detected speech, protecting quiet first and last words.
- Brief utterances are safely padded for Parakeet instead of being rejected
  when they are shorter than one second.
- Long meetings use natural pause boundaries where possible and overlapping,
  deduplicated 25-second sections when speech continues without a pause.
- Final audio held by the sample-rate converter is drained when recording
  stops, and a failed Parakeet section is retried once.
- Meeting transcripts now apply the same `Vocabulary.md` replacements as
  polished Quick Dictation.
- Capture begins accepting audio before the microphone and system-audio
  streams start, preventing an immediate greeting from being clipped.

Long recordings remain memory-bounded, and all speech recognition remains on
the Mac. Audio is not uploaded for transcription.
