# Yaprflow 5.2.3

This release completes the unified workflow for meetings and quick dictations:

- Meetings and dictations now share one output catalog containing General
  Summary, 1:1 Notes, Interview Notes, Research Synthesis, Sales Follow-up,
  Standup Update, Action Plan, Follow-up Email, and Detailed Notes.
- Every output prompt can be reviewed, customized, or reset from Settings.
- Meeting transcripts more reliably remove duplicated Mac playback from the
  microphone track, including when the two recognition streams phrase the same
  audio differently or omit different words.
- Transcript ordering and timestamps are consistent, and follow-up generation
  is grounded so missing contact details are not invented.
- Meetings and quick dictations share a cleaner Library and can both be deleted
  from their history.

Quick Dictation and Meeting Notes continue to transcribe locally, and raw
meeting audio is not retained.
