Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Summary

Yaprflow is designed to keep your dictation private. The macOS and iOS apps do not require a Yaprflow account and do not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. The apps do not send your audio, transcripts, or generated text to the developer.

Microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Yaprflow accesses the microphone only while you are recording. Audio is processed on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad using speech-recognition models included with the app. Raw microphone audio is not retained after transcription and is not sent to Yaprflow or to a developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

On macOS, completed transcript text is copied to the system clipboard and stored as Markdown files in Yaprflow's sandboxed app container. Those files may include timestamps, the selected dictation mode, and the name of the application that was active during dictation. Yaprflow also stores your local vocabulary, keyboard shortcut, settings, most recent transcript, and custom AI prompt on your Mac.

On iOS, completed transcript text is copied to the system clipboard and up to three recent transcripts are stored in the app's local preferences for quick access.

This local data remains under your control and is not transmitted to the developer.

Apple Intelligence

On supported Macs, optional AI Summary and transcript metadata features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Yaprflow does not send the transcript or generated result to a developer-operated server. Availability and processing are governed by your macOS and Apple Intelligence settings.

Permissions and deletion

On macOS, you can revoke microphone access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. The Folder button in History reveals local transcript files so you can review or delete them. To remove all macOS data, quit and uninstall Yaprflow, then delete its sandbox container.

On iOS, you can revoke microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Choosing Delete App, rather than Offload App, removes the app and its locally stored data.

App Store purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases, payment information, and Apple Account activity under Apple's Privacy Policy. Yaprflow does not create a separate account and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide the developer with transaction and sales information under its App Store terms.

Support email

If you contact support, you voluntarily send the developer your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. That information is used to respond to your request and may be retained as needed to resolve it or meet legal and security obligations. You do not need to send a transcript or recording to receive support.

Website

The Yaprflow website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-request information according to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Yaprflow is not directed to children. The apps do not transmit personal information to the developer. If a child contacts support, the information in that message is used only to respond, protect the service, or comply with applicable law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tim@yaprflow.com. General help is available on the support page.
