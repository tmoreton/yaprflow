Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

Summary

Yaprflow is designed to keep your dictation private. The apps do not include advertising or cross-app tracking. The Mac app shares limited usage and error counts by default; you can turn this off in Settings. The apps do not send your audio, transcripts, or generated text to the developer.

Microphone, system audio, and speech recognition

With your permission, Yaprflow accesses the microphone only while you are recording. Meeting Notes can also capture Mac system audio after you explicitly start a meeting and grant Screen & System Audio Recording access. Audio is processed on your device using speech-recognition models included with the app. Raw microphone and system audio are not retained after transcription and are not sent to Yaprflow or to a developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

On macOS, completed dictation text is copied to the system clipboard and stored as Markdown files in Yaprflow's sandboxed app container. Meeting Notes stores JSON and Markdown records containing the meeting title, timestamps, typed notes, transcript, generated notes, evidence identifiers, and any selected calendar event context such as attendee names. Yaprflow also stores your local vocabulary, keyboard shortcut, settings, most recent transcript, and custom AI prompt on your Mac.

On iOS, completed transcript text is copied to the system clipboard and up to three recent transcripts are stored in the app's local preferences for quick access.

This local data remains under your control and is not transmitted to the developer.

Optional AI providers on Mac

Apple Intelligence remains the default for optional AI Summary and transcript metadata on supported Macs. It uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. You may instead select OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Ollama in Settings.

When you run AI Summary or cross-meeting questions with OpenAI or OpenRouter, Yaprflow sends the selected transcript or relevant meeting excerpts, your prompt, and task instructions directly from your Mac to that provider. When one of these providers is configured, stopping Meeting Notes automatically sends that meeting transcript and typed notes to it to generate structured notes. The provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy and may charge your account. Your API key is saved in the Mac Keychain, not in Yaprflow preferences or on a Yaprflow server.

Automatic archive titles using a selected OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Ollama model are off by default. If you enable them, each new Mac transcript is sent to that model for its title, topic, and description. Existing saved transcripts are not automatically uploaded to a cloud provider for backfill.

Ollama requests go to the Ollama service at localhost:11434. A local Ollama model processes them on your Mac; an Ollama cloud model may send them to Ollama's service. Yaprflow does not send your AI requests or API keys to a developer-operated server.

Mac usage telemetry

The Mac app sends usage telemetry to Aptabase by default unless you turn it off in Settings. Events cover app launches, a possible unexpected exit of the previous run, opening major features, dictation starts and outcomes, AI Summary use, archive-title failures, and opening a feedback email draft. Failure details use fixed categories, and recording durations are broad ranges. An interrupted run can also result from a forced quit or power loss; it is not a crash report.

Events include the app version and build, macOS major and minor version, and a random session identifier that changes when the app launches or after an hour of inactivity. Yaprflow does not send a persistent device identifier, audio, transcript or generated text, AI prompts, vocabulary, feedback messages, the active app name, or free-form error text. Turning telemetry off stops new events and cancels pending requests. The telemetry service receives ordinary network information needed to process requests; see Aptabase's privacy policy.

Telemetry is only available in Mac builds configured with an Aptabase app key. It is not included in the iOS source app.

Permissions and deletion

On macOS, you can revoke microphone, calendar, and Screen & System Audio Recording access in System Settings > Privacy & Security. The Folder and Reveal actions expose local transcript and meeting files so you can review or delete them. To remove all macOS data, quit and uninstall Yaprflow, then delete its sandbox container.

On iOS, you can revoke microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Choosing Delete App, rather than Offload App, removes the app and its locally stored data.

Direct downloads

The paid Mac download is not yet for sale. Purchase and private download providers will be identified here before checkout opens. The Mac app itself does not require sign-in.

Support email

The in-app feedback form prepares an email draft with your message, Yaprflow version, and operating-system version. It does not attach a transcript or recording, and nothing is sent until you review and send the draft. If you contact support, you voluntarily send the developer your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. That information is used to respond to your request and may be retained as needed to resolve it or meet legal and security obligations. You do not need to send a transcript or recording to receive support.

Website

The Yaprflow website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-request information according to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Yaprflow is not directed to children. The apps do not transmit personal information to the developer. If a child contacts support, the information in that message is used only to respond, protect the service, or comply with applicable law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tim@yaprflow.com. General help is available on the support page.
