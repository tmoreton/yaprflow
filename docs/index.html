Private by default

Built for dictation you would not send to a server.

Yaprflow keeps speech recognition on-device. There are no accounts, no telemetry, and no remote transcription queue, so meeting notes, names, client details, and rough first drafts stay under your control.

  • Audio stays on your Mac
  • Transcripts are saved locally as Markdown
  • Custom vocabulary lives in a plain local file

Simple workflow

Speak once. Use it anywhere.

01

Press the hotkey

Start and stop dictation from the menu bar with Command T.

02

Talk naturally

Live partials show what Yaprflow hears while filler words and common dictation noise are cleaned up.

03

Paste clean text

Your final transcript lands on the clipboard, ready for email, chat, notes, issues, and docs.

Why it feels different

Fast dictation without the cloud tradeoff.

On-device speech model

NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 runs locally through Core ML for quick, accurate transcription.

Cleaner defaults

Common filler words and rough speech patterns are removed so your pasted text needs less cleanup.

Local vocabulary

Add deterministic phrase replacements for names, technical terms, acronyms, and preferred spellings.

Transcript archive

Every completed dictation can be saved as a Markdown record with useful local metadata.

Multilingual support

Dictate across 25 European languages with automatic language detection.

Quiet menu bar app

Yaprflow stays out of the way until you need to turn speech into text.
