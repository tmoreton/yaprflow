Talk to a person.

Email Tim at tim@yaprflow.com, or open Settings in the Mac app and choose Send Feedback. The form prepares an email with your message, app version, and operating-system version for you to review and send. It never attaches a transcript or recording.

Get back to your words.

Install your download

After Stripe confirms your payment, the confirmation page provides your private Mac download. Open the disk image, drag Yaprflow to Applications, then open the app and allow microphone access. No App Store account or Yaprflow sign-in is required.

Find a purchase or download

Return to your confirmation page in the same browser within 30 days; a necessary purchase cookie remembers your access. If you clear site data, use another browser, or the cookie expires, email tim@yaprflow.com with the email used at checkout. Contact us too if payment completed but your download is not ready. Declining optional tracking does not affect purchase access, and the 30-day browser cookie does not limit use of your app. Direct website refund requests are accepted within 14 days. Please do not send card details. See the purchase, license, and refund terms for details.

Allow microphone access

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and make sure Yaprflow is enabled. Press Command-T to start recording and press it again to finish. Your text is copied to the clipboard, ready to paste.

Change the keyboard shortcut

Another app may already use Command-T. Open Yaprflow Settings, click the shortcut button, and press a different key combination. Press Escape to cancel shortcut capture.

The first dictation takes longer

Yaprflow prepares its recognizer in the background at launch. Recording before that finishes can take longer. The recognizer also releases memory after five idle minutes or memory pressure, so a later recording may need time to prepare again.

Choose the right speech language

Open Settings and choose the language you plan to speak. English (United States) is the default. Automatic can detect among the 32 supported locales; choosing one language usually helps short or noisy speech stay in that language.

AI Summary is unavailable

Apple Intelligence requires macOS 26 or later, an eligible Mac, and Apple Intelligence enabled in System Settings. You can also choose OpenAI or OpenRouter with your own API key, or select an installed Ollama model in Settings. Cloud providers may charge your account. Core dictation works without an AI provider.

Find a recent transcript

Open History from the menu-bar app to browse and copy saved transcripts. Use Folder to reveal your local Markdown files. You can open, back up, or delete those files yourself.

Check your Mac.

  • The Mac download requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and supports Intel and Apple silicon.
  • Apple Intelligence features require macOS 26 or later on a supported Mac, with Apple Intelligence enabled.
  • OpenAI, OpenRouter, and Ollama can be selected on earlier supported macOS versions. Dictation, local history, and vocabulary work without an AI provider.

Using an iPhone or iPad source build?

The separate iPhone and iPad source app requires iOS 17 or later. Enable its microphone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, tap the clock to see up to three recent transcripts, and use its feedback button to prepare a support email. There is no Mac/iOS sync. The website purchase is for the Mac download.

Your words stay in your control.

Dictation runs locally, and raw audio is discarded after transcription. Optional cloud AI providers receive the transcript and prompt when you use them. Mac usage telemetry starts on and can be turned off in Settings; it never includes audio, transcripts, or prompts. Read the privacy policy for the details.
