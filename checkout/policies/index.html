Offer & payment terms

The founding offer for Yaprflow for Mac is a one-time US $7.99 purchase, plus applicable tax. It is not a subscription and does not renew automatically. Stripe Checkout shows the final amount and any tax before you pay.

Your purchase lets you keep using the downloaded app without recurring Yaprflow payments. “Lifetime” describes continued use of the purchased app; it does not promise all future products or features. Promotional prices may change for future buyers.

The app includes unlimited local dictation, clean and exact text modes, transcript history, custom vocabulary, and AI Summary with a configurable prompt. Local dictation works on Intel and Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Apple Intelligence is the default AI provider on eligible Macs with macOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence enabled. You can also choose OpenAI, OpenRouter, or an installed Ollama model in Settings. Those providers have their own requirements and terms, and cloud services may charge your account separately.

Payment & private download

Stripe handles checkout and payment information. Yaprflow’s checkout server verifies the payment with Stripe before providing access to the Mac download, stored privately with Vercel Blob. Stripe provides the purchaser’s email address so Yaprflow can deliver the purchase, locate it for support or refunds, prevent duplicate delivery, and meet legal or security obligations. See Stripe’s privacy policy and Vercel’s privacy policy.

After Stripe confirms payment, the confirmation page provides your download and Resend sends a private purchase-recovery link to the checkout email address. That link rechecks the purchase with Stripe before restoring browser access and never exposes the temporary installer address. Treat it like a receipt and do not forward it. See Resend’s privacy policy.

A necessary purchase cookie lets you return to the confirmation page in the same browser for 30 days. The emailed link can restore access in another browser or on another Mac. If the message does not arrive, contact hello@yaprflow.com from the checkout email address. This browser-access period does not limit use of the purchased Mac app. The app does not require a Yaprflow account or in-app sign-in.

If you purchase Yaprflow through the Mac App Store, Apple processes that purchase and provides its download, billing support, and applicable refund process. The price and terms shown by Apple apply to that purchase. Mac App Store purchases do not create access to the website's Stripe download, and website purchases do not create Mac App Store ownership.

When checkout is labeled “Test mode,” no real charge is made. Test payment details are for testing the payment and download process and are not a live purchase.

App privacy

Summary

Yaprflow is designed to keep your dictation private. The apps do not include advertising or cross-app tracking. The Mac app shares limited usage and error counts by default; you can turn this off in Settings. The apps do not send your audio, transcripts, or generated text to the developer.

Microphone, system audio, and speech recognition

With your permission, Yaprflow accesses the microphone only while you are recording. Meeting Notes can also capture Mac system audio after you explicitly start a meeting and grant Screen & System Audio Recording access. Audio is processed on your device using speech-recognition models included with the app. Raw microphone and system audio are not retained after transcription and are not sent to Yaprflow or to a developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

On macOS, completed dictation text is copied to the system clipboard and stored as Markdown files in Yaprflow's sandboxed app container. Meeting Notes stores JSON and Markdown records containing the meeting title, timestamps, typed notes, transcript, generated notes, and evidence identifiers. Yaprflow also stores your local vocabulary, keyboard shortcut, settings, most recent transcript, and custom AI prompt on your Mac.

On iOS, Dictation copies completed transcript text to the system clipboard and stores up to 50 recent results in the app's local preferences. Meeting stores the meeting title, timestamps, typed notes, notes format, and transcript as local JSON and Markdown files. The iOS app uses only the device microphone and does not capture audio from other apps. Mac and iOS records do not sync in this release.

This local data remains under your control and is not transmitted to the developer.

Optional AI providers on Mac

Apple Intelligence remains the default for optional AI Summary and transcript metadata on supported Macs. It uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. You may instead select OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Ollama in Settings.

When you run transcript tools or ask a question about a selected meeting with OpenAI or OpenRouter, Yaprflow sends that selected transcript or meeting excerpts, your prompt, and task instructions directly from your Mac to that provider. When one of these providers is configured, stopping Meeting Notes automatically sends that meeting transcript and typed notes to it to generate structured notes. The provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy and may charge your account. Your API key is saved in the Mac Keychain, not in Yaprflow preferences or on a Yaprflow server.

Automatic archive titles using a selected OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Ollama model are off by default. If you enable them, each new Mac transcript is sent to that model for its title, topic, and description. Existing saved transcripts are not automatically uploaded to a cloud provider for backfill.

Ollama requests go to the Ollama service at localhost:11434. A local Ollama model processes them on your Mac; an Ollama cloud model may send them to Ollama's service. Yaprflow does not send your AI requests or API keys to a developer-operated server.

Mac usage telemetry

The Mac app sends usage telemetry to Aptabase by default unless you turn it off in Settings. Events cover app launches, a possible unexpected exit of the previous run, opening major features, dictation starts and outcomes, AI Summary use, archive-title failures, and opening a feedback email draft. Failure details use fixed categories, and recording durations are broad ranges. An interrupted run can also result from a forced quit or power loss; it is not a crash report.

Events include the app version and build, macOS major and minor version, and a random session identifier that changes when the app launches or after an hour of inactivity. Yaprflow does not send a persistent device identifier, audio, transcript or generated text, AI prompts, vocabulary, feedback messages, the active app name, or free-form error text. Turning telemetry off stops new events and cancels pending requests. The telemetry service receives ordinary network information needed to process requests; see Aptabase's privacy policy.

Telemetry is only available in Mac builds configured with an Aptabase app key. It is not included in the iOS source app.

Software updates

The website edition checks Yaprflow's update feed about once a day by default and can download eligible updates automatically. Update requests contain the information ordinarily needed to deliver a file, such as your IP address and app version. Sparkle's optional system-profile reporting is disabled. You can turn automatic checks or downloads off in Settings and check manually at any time.

The Mac App Store edition does not include Yaprflow's updater or contact its update feed. Apple delivers updates for that edition according to your Mac App Store settings.

Permissions and deletion

On macOS, you can revoke microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording access in System Settings > Privacy & Security. The Folder and Reveal actions expose local transcript and meeting files so you can review or delete them. To remove all macOS data, quit and uninstall Yaprflow, then delete its sandbox container.

On iOS, you can revoke microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Choosing Delete App, rather than Offload App, removes the app and its locally stored data.

Website & browser demo

The optional browser microphone demo uses cloud transcription. It is not the local Mac app. Playing the prerecorded demonstration does not activate your microphone.

When you choose “Start talking”

Your browser asks for microphone permission. If you grant it and start the demo, your audio is sent to OpenAI’s transcription service to generate text. The text is displayed in the current browser page. You can stop recording and revoke microphone permission in your browser at any time.

The website server establishes the transcription session; it does not save your demo audio or transcript. The demo transcript is not stored by this page across reloads. If you copy it, a copy remains on your clipboard according to your device’s behavior. Provider-side handling is governed by OpenAI’s privacy policy and its API data controls; the Mac app’s local-processing promise does not apply to this website demo.

The browser experiment is free to visitors and has abuse-prevention limits. Those website limits do not apply to the Mac app’s unlimited word usage.

Website delivery and logs

Vercel hosts this website. Like other website infrastructure, hosting services receive connection details such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request timing to deliver the site and maintain its security. The browser demo uses your connection address for temporary rate limiting.

The landing page requests fonts from Google Fonts. That service receives the network information necessary to respond to the request. The demonstration video is served from this website.

Necessary purchase cookie

When Stripe returns you to the website, the server stores your private checkout reference in a purchase cookie and redirects you to a confirmation page whose address contains no checkout reference. Older confirmation links are handled the same way before the page loads. The cookie lasts 30 days, is limited to the host that set it, and is sent only over a secure connection. Page scripts cannot read it. The server checks the purchase with Stripe each time you request its status or download.

This cookie supports access to your purchase and is separate from analytics or advertising cookies. Declining or withdrawing optional tracking does not remove it. Clearing it removes this browser’s saved access, so contact support if you need help returning to your download.

Optional analytics, marketing, and your choice

The Yaprflow purchase form offers a separate optional choice to receive product news. That choice is recorded with the Stripe purchase. When newsletter delivery is enabled, only purchasers who selected the checkbox are added to Yaprflow’s newsletter segment in Resend. Buying the app does not subscribe you. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time; an unsubscribe does not remove your Stripe purchase record or prevent purchase-support messages. This newsletter choice is separate from the website cookie settings below.

Google Analytics and Meta marketing tracking are enabled by default on yaprflow.com and www.yaprflow.com when you have no saved tracking choice. We do not show an automatic cookie prompt. Open “Cookie settings” in the footer to turn tracking off or change it: “Turn off tracking” turns both categories off, “Analytics only” keeps Google Analytics on and Meta off, and “Allow all” enables both. Your saved choices, including opt-outs, continue to apply on later visits. You can turn tracking off and still use the website, buy the app, and access your download. These website settings are separate from the Mac app’s usage telemetry.

While analytics is enabled, our website sends Google page views, offer clicks, demonstration-video starts and completions, checkout starts, verified purchases, and download-button clicks. The events sent by our code use a canonical page path without query parameters, fragments, or referring URLs, and predefined fields such as purchase currency, amount, tax, and a hashed transaction reference. A purchase event is sent only after the server verifies a paid live purchase with Stripe.

Google’s automatic Enhanced Measurement is also enabled. Depending on the interaction, it can measure additional activity such as scrolling, outbound links, file-download links, and form interactions, including related link or form information. Collection is therefore not limited to the custom events listed above. We have kept Google’s email redaction enabled and configured URL query-parameter redaction for checkout session references, signed-download credentials, client secrets, payment references, tokens, and signatures.

While marketing is enabled, the Meta Pixel uses advertising cookies and browser activity to help measure Yaprflow advertisements and attribute visits and purchases. We send page views, a landing-page content view, an event when an available live checkout is submitted, and a purchase event only after the server verifies a paid live purchase. Purchase details are limited to the actual amount excluding tax, currency, and a hashed event reference. Meta’s automatic configuration and advanced matching are disabled for this integration.

The custom events written for this website exclude email addresses, audio, transcripts, checkout session identifiers, and private download URLs. The confirmation page loads only after its checkout reference has been removed from the address; private signed download addresses are handled by the server and are not placed in the page. Google and Meta receive ordinary connection and browser information needed to provide their services, and their optional cookies can recognize a browser. Google advertising signals, advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization remain disabled even when Meta marketing is enabled.

Choices you make in “Cookie settings” are saved in your browser’s local storage. The initial default is not saved as a choice you made. Google Analytics and Meta cookies are used while their respective categories are enabled. The website does not save purchase references in local storage; the necessary purchase cookie is separate. Turning a category off stops its further collection and clears its cookies; it does not remove events already sent or the necessary purchase cookie.

The page does not store your microphone transcript in browser local storage. Your browser may cache website files, and service providers may retain operational and analytics data under their own settings and policies. Clicking an ad on another platform is subject to that platform’s privacy practices.

Website providers may process connection information in countries other than yours. See Vercel’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for their practices.
