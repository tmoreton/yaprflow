Yaprflow bundles the 1120 ms chunk-size INT8 encoder, decoder, joiner, and
token table without modifying their contents. The source model supports 40
language-locales across 35 languages: 32 locales transcribe out of the box and
8 adaptation-ready locales require fine-tuning. Yaprflow exposes automatic
language detection for the 32 production-ready locales.

The export repository links to the NVIDIA source model but does not record the
exact NVIDIA commit used for conversion. The exported binaries are pinned by
the export revision, official archive SHA-256, and per-file SHA-256 checksums.
